# The Universal Process

Every project in every field runs through the same six units, in the same order, on the same weekly rhythm. This chapter is what a chapter leader actually needs to memorize — it stays the same length no matter how many fields are running in the chapter this season.

## Unit I — Learning Innovation Challenge
**Weeks 1–4 · Artifact: Innovation Proposal I**

- Week 1: Readings + pre-reading reflection + discussion.
- Week 2: **Game Tournament — The Confidence Gap.** Every team runs a five-minute station from its own field; the whole chapter competes on one leaderboard, scored by smallest gap between predicted and actual performance.
- Week 3: Field-in-Action experimental design (Prediction → Trial 1 → Trial 2 → Trial 3 → Reflection → Interpretation).
- Week 4: Main output — Learning Innovation Challenge.

## Unit II — Research Investigation
**Weeks 5–8 · Artifact: Research Investigation / Prospectus**

- Week 5: Readings + Research Prospectus + Research Notebook launch.
- Week 6: **Game Tournament — The Prediction Ladder.** Every team prepares one ambiguous or counter-intuitive case from its field; participants predict the outcome and rate their own confidence before the reveal.
- Week 7: **Cognitive Laboratory — Build-a-System Simulation.** Every team runs its own five-stage simulation (Reception → Filtering → Integration → Reconstruction → Decision) using its own project's stimulus, then the chapter reconvenes for a shared cross-team debrief.
- Week 8: Main output — Research Investigation.

## Unit III — Public Communication & Social Impact
**Weeks 9–12 · Artifact: Communication Product + Dissemination Plan**

- Week 9: Readings + Science Communication Objective.
- Week 10: **Game Tournament — Signal and the Noise.** Every team prepares one piece of content framed two different ways with identical underlying facts; participants rate their reaction to each framing.
- Week 11: **Cognitive Laboratory — Message Transmission Simulation.** Five roles (Message Creator → Deliverer → Filterer → Reconstructor → Decision-Maker) run per team, then a shared cross-team debrief.
- Week 12: Main output — Public Communication & Social Impact Project.

## Unit IV — Memory/Decision Systems Simulation
**Weeks 13–16 · Artifact: Simulation Model IV**

- Week 13: Readings + Systems Thinking Objective.
- Week 14: **Game Tournament — The Distortion Chain.** Every team's short piece of field content is shown, recalled immediately, then recalled again after a delay; gap and confidence are both scored.
- Week 15: **Cognitive Laboratory — Distortion & Reconstruction Experiment.** Observation → Initial Recall → Information Manipulation → Final Recall → Comparison → Reflection, run per team, then a shared cross-team debrief.
- Week 16: Main output — Memory/Decision Systems Simulation Project.

## Unit V — Policy Formation Simulation
**Weeks 17–20 · Artifact: Policy Draft Document**

- Week 17: Readings + Policy Literacy Objective.
- Week 18: **Game Tournament — The Self-Forecast Duel.** Every team seals three real predictions about its own project's near-term future, reveals them two weeks later, and is scored on accuracy.
- Week 19: **Cognitive Laboratory — Identity & System Construction Workshop.** Five mapping components (Origin, Influence, Stakeholder, Environmental, System) applied to the team's own project.
- Week 20: Main output — Policy Formation Simulation (Draft → Peer Review → Revision → Defense).

## Unit VI — Integration & Summit
**Weeks 21–24 · Artifact: Capstone Project**

- Week 21: Analysis → Synthesis → Creation, Integration Lab, five Design Tracks.
- Week 22: **Cognitive Laboratory — Capstone Prototype Development.** Idea Selection → Evidence Mapping → Prototype Construction → Feedback Loop, run individually per project team.
- Week 23: Capstone Project Draft.
- Week 24: **Axon Review Summit.** Portfolio Exhibition, Capstone Presentations, Experimental Showcase, Policy/Communication Presentation, Reflection Defense — same five components for every project, every field.

## What Never Varies

The tournament formats, the laboratory formats, the artifact names, and the evaluation criteria are fixed across the entire organization. **Only the literature and the project content change by field** — that is the one and only thing a chapter leader needs to source separately per team, and it's exactly what the field chapters in this book already provide.
