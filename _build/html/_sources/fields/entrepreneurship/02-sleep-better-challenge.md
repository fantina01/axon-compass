## Core Literature — Read This Before Starting Unit II

Real, verified sources for this project. Read these before Unit II begins — they're what your literature review and hypothesis should be built on.

### 1. Carskadon, M. A. (2011). Sleep in adolescents: The perfect storm. Pediatric Clinics of North America, 58(3), 637-647.

(Verified in the Education field's "Sleep-Deprived School Start" project.) Carskadon's research establishes that adolescents experience a genuine biological delay in their circadian rhythm — directly relevant to why a peer sleep challenge needs to work with, not against, this biological reality.

### 2. Irish, L. A., Kline, C. E., Gunn, H. E., Buysse, D. J., & Hall, M. H. (2015). The role of sleep hygiene in promoting public health: A review of empirical evidence. Sleep Medicine Reviews, 22, 23-36.

This review evaluated the actual evidence behind common "sleep hygiene" recommendations (consistent bedtime, reduced screen time before bed), finding real but modest support for several specific practices — useful, practical guidance for exactly which actions the challenge's program should actually include, based on which specific recommendations have real supporting evidence rather than folk wisdom alone.

### 3. Owens, J., & Adolescent Sleep Working Group (2014). Insufficient sleep in adolescents and young adults: An update on causes and consequences. Pediatrics, 134(3), e921-e932.

This clinical report reviews the real consequences of insufficient adolescent sleep on mood, cognitive performance, and health, and recommends practical, achievable behavior changes for improving sleep in this age group specifically. This is a useful, medically-grounded reference for both the science behind the challenge and realistic, age-appropriate recommendations.

### Unit I — Learning Innovation Challenge
*Weeks 1–4 · Required artifact: **Innovation Proposal I***

**Process for this unit (same for every project in the chapter):** Run the Week 2 **Confidence Gap** tournament (see the Universal Process chapter).

**Steps specific to this project:**

- [ ] Students design and launch a real "Sleep Better Challenge" — a simple one-week program peers can sign up for, with a few concrete, science-backed actions (consistent bedtime, less screen time before bed) and a way to check in daily. Innovation Proposal I documents the program design and how peers will sign up.

### Unit II — Research Investigation
*Weeks 5–8 · Required artifact: **Research Investigation / Prospectus***

**Process for this unit (same for every project in the chapter):** Week 6 **Prediction Ladder** tournament + Week 7 **Build-a-System** laboratory.

**Steps specific to this project:**

- [ ] **Literature review:** sleep-and-brain-function research, explained simply as the reason behind each program action.
- [ ] **Hypothesis:** peers who complete the challenge will report better sleep and better mood/focus at the end of the week than at the start.
- [ ] **Variables:** before-and-after self-ratings for the same participants.
- [ ] **Methodology:** have real participants rate their sleep and focus before starting and again after finishing, and compare.
