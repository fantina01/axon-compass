This is the single place to find, start, and finish any Axon Review project — no matter which field a team picked. It answers three questions for any participant or chapter leader, at any point in the season:

1. **What exactly do I need to do right now, this week, for my specific project?**
2. **What literature do I need to have read before I start?**
3. **How does what I'm doing this week connect to everything else in my project?**

## How This Book Is Organized

- {doc}`process` — the six-unit season structure every project follows, and the five reusable tournament/laboratory formats every chapter leader runs regardless of field. Read this once, at the start of the season.
- **Fields** (left sidebar) — eight fields, ten complete project pathways each. Every project page gives the exact steps for all six units, the literature to read first, and how the six outputs become one portfolio.

## For Chapter Leaders

You do not need to learn 80 projects. You need to learn:

- **Six units**, run in the same order every season.
- **Five tournament formats**, one per unit that has a tournament, reused across every field in your chapter.
- **Five laboratory formats**, one per unit that has a lab, reused across every field in your chapter.

Every team in your chapter — regardless of field — plugs its own project's content into that same fixed shape. The project pages in this book are where each team finds exactly what to plug in.

## For Participants

Open your field, find your project, and work top to bottom through the checklist for whichever unit you're currently in. Check off each step as your team completes it — this checklist *is* the backbone of your Cognitive Portfolio.
